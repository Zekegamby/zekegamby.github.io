Tested range, requirements and remaining checks

What the numbers mean

For a 12 mm cube, each pad reference must be within 0.3 mm of an opposing face, within 15° of its normal and at least 1.5 mm inside the face edge. Both nail sides must face away from the target. A green result means these geometric checks pass at the solved pose.

Workspace test results loading…

The six preset approach paths passed sampled rigid hand–box and hand-part clearance checks. The wrist mechanism and forearm cable routing are excluded. Soft surface overlap reaches 0.97 mm in these paths; this does not establish actual pad compression or holding force.

Joint targets use four finger commands with assumed curl ratios. They are not calibrated servo commands or a tendon dynamics result. The box position is supplied by these controls; camera tracking is not included.
