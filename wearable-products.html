Overview

This project explores wearable and interactive products that respond to the body and its movement. The work investigates how light, motion, and material can be integrated into wearable forms to create dynamic visual experiences.

Long-exposure photography was used to document the light trails produced during use, revealing the invisible pathways of motion embedded in the design. The process blurs the boundary between product photography and performance documentation.
