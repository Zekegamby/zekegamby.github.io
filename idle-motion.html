Idle Motion

A study of fidgeting, everyday habit and movement, explored through a cultural probe and a kinetic sculpture.

Discipline
Design research
Methods
Cultural probe · Observation
Outputs
Zine · Kinetic sculpture

01 / The question

What do our hands do
when we’re not thinking?

Idle Motion investigates fidgeting through two related studies: a cultural probe into everyday tactile habits, and a kinetic sculpture that gives repetitive movement a physical form.

The work moves between observing ordinary behaviour and making objects that help us notice it.

02 / Cultural probe

Small objects.
Individual responses.

Participants were given fidget objects to use, rank and respond to over several days. Their choices and observations became material for a zine about habit, attention and tactile preference.

The printed pages bring the objects, rankings and written responses together so the study can be read as a record of interaction.

03 / Kinetic study

Repetition made visible.

The second part translates fidgeting into a rotating arrangement of fingers. A continuous circular motion turns an often-unnoticed habit into something that can be watched and studied.
