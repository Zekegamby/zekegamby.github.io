About Me

I'm an industrial designer passionate about creating innovative solutions that bridge technology and human needs. My work focuses on accessible design, advanced manufacturing, and sustainable product development.

With expertise in CAD, prototyping, and user-centered design, I bring ideas from concept to reality through careful research, iteration, and attention to detail.
