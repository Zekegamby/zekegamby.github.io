How I work

The interesting part is
what happens next.

01 / Design

Make a possibility.

Use sketches, CAD and AI to explore what a mechanism could become. Give the idea a physical form worth testing.

02 / Test

Find the limit.

Pull it, bend it, load it. A joint can look right on screen and still tear, resist movement or lose force.

03 / Learn

Change the next one.

Let the result shape the next question. The prototype is a way to learn how the design actually behaves.

About / Approach

I work things out
by making them.

Sketch, model, build, test. My work moves between digital tools and physical prototypes, with each experiment shaping the next.

Originally from Kauaʻi, I’m now based in Wellington, studying for a Master of Design Innovation at Victoria University of Wellington.

  • Industrial design & CAD
  • Physical prototyping & mechanisms
  • Multi-material 3D printing
