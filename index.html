The research

A physical
partner for AI.

Designing a robot’s shape and materials together.

I’m developing a modified PolyJet workflow so AI-assisted designs can specify both a robot’s shape and where rigid and flexible materials go. This connects design decisions more directly to what gets printed.

Robotic hands are my test case. The aim is to reduce fabrication and assembly steps, then use physical testing to improve the next design.

Where I am now

I’ve progressed from individual fingers to the thumb-and-index prototype shown above. I’m testing joint movement, tendon routing and tension.

I currently direct the changes and carry out the tests. The longer-term goal is a cycle of simulation, printing, testing and redesign, with AI guiding more of the decisions.
