The research

A physical partner
for AI.

AI can help design a robot’s body as well as how it moves. My research explores what happens when its geometry, joints and hard and soft regions can be designed together and produced in one print.

PolyJet gives me a way to test that idea. I’m developing a cycle of design, simulation, printing and physical testing, using what happens on the bench to improve the next version.

Explore the research ↗

The research process

The interesting part is
what happens next.

01 / Design & simulate

Make a possibility.

Use AI, CAD and simulation to explore geometry, joints and movement before committing to a physical prototype.

02 / Print & test

Find the limit.

Print hard and soft regions together, then put the mechanism to work. Look for friction, tearing, unwanted movement and loss of force.

03 / Learn & redesign

Change the next one.

Use the physical results to change the next design. I currently direct the iterations and carry out the testing myself.
