Overview

Order in Chaos is an art piece that investigates digital manufacturing techniques through the lens of modular assembly and controlled disorder. Each component is precisely fabricated yet deliberately assembled in ways that introduce unpredictability.

The work asks: at what point does order become chaos, and what does it mean to design a system that embraces both? The piece uses material repetition and structural variation to create a visual tension between grid and entropy.
